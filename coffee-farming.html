☕ Complete Coffee Production Guide

🌍 Introduction

Coffee is one of Africa's most valuable cash crops, providing income for millions of farming households and contributing significantly to national economies.

Producing high-quality coffee requires selecting suitable varieties, planting in the right environment, maintaining healthy trees, and harvesting ripe cherries at the proper time. Good management practices increase yields and improve bean quality and market value.

☕ Coffee Production Information

Production Factor Recommendation
🌡️ Suitable Climate Warm humid climate: Arabica 18–24°C and Robusta 24–30°C.
⛰️ Altitude Arabica: 1,000–2,200 m. Robusta: 0–900 m above sea level.
🌱 Soil Type Deep, fertile, well-drained loam soil rich in organic matter.
⚖️ Soil pH 5.5–6.5
🌧️ Rainfall Requirement 1,200–2,000 mm per year, well distributed.
📅 Planting Season Beginning of the rainy season.
📏 Spacing Arabica: 2m × 2m. Robusta: 3m × 3m.
🌿 First Harvest 2–4 years after planting.
📈 Peak Production 5–8 years after planting.
🌳 Productive Lifespan 20–30 years with good management.
🪴 Fertilizer Use compost, manure, and balanced fertilizers based on soil testing.
✂️ Pruning Remove old and diseased branches to encourage healthy growth.
🐛 Common Pests Coffee berry borer, leaf miners, and mealybugs.
🍂 Diseases Coffee leaf rust, coffee berry disease, and wilt disease.
🧺 Harvesting Pick only fully ripe red cherries.
📦 Drying Dry beans to about 10–12% moisture before storage.
📊 Expected Yield 1–3 tonnes of clean coffee per hectare under good management.

🌍 Why Coffee Is Important

💰 Farmer Income

Coffee provides a dependable source of income for farming families.

🌎 Global Trade

Coffee is one of the world's most traded agricultural commodities.

👨‍🌾 Employment

Creates jobs in farming, processing, transportation and export.

🌳 Long-Term Investment

Well-managed coffee farms can remain productive for decades.

🚀 Tips for Higher Coffee Yields

🌱 Quality Varieties

Select high-yielding and disease-resistant coffee varieties.

💧 Soil Management

Apply organic matter to improve fertility and moisture retention.

🐛 Pest Control

Monitor farms regularly and control pests and diseases early.

☕ Quality Harvest

Harvest ripe cherries and dry properly to maintain premium quality.
