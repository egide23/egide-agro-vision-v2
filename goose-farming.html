What is Goose Farming? 🪿

Goose farming is the practice of raising domesticated geese for meat, eggs, breeding stock, feathers, and other useful products. Geese are hardy birds that can make good use of pasture and vegetation when properly managed.

Successful goose production depends on suitable housing, balanced nutrition, clean water, good hygiene, appropriate breeding management, disease prevention, and careful records.

02

Why Raise Geese?

Meat Production

Geese can be raised for meat and may provide a valuable poultry product for local and specialty markets.

Natural Grazing

Geese can consume grasses and other vegetation, allowing farmers to integrate grazing into their production system.

Egg Production

Goose eggs are larger than chicken eggs and can be produced for consumption or hatching.

Farm Integration

Goose production can be integrated with crop and livestock enterprises where land, water, and feed resources are suitable.

03

Types of Goose Production

🥩 Meat Production

Focuses on efficient growth, healthy birds, appropriate feeding, and good carcass quality.

🥚 Egg Production

Focuses on productive females, proper nutrition, nesting areas, egg collection, and breeding management.

🐣 Breeding Production

Breeding farms maintain healthy males and females and manage mating, fertile eggs, incubation, and gosling production.

🌱 Pasture-Based Production

Geese are allowed access to suitable pasture while receiving supplementary feed and adequate shelter.

04

Goose Breeds & Types

Goose breeds differ in body size, growth rate, egg production, temperament, grazing ability, and adaptation to local conditions.

Type Common Characteristics Potential Use
Embden Large-bodied and commonly associated with meat production. Meat
Toulouse Large breed with a heavy body and calm characteristics. Meat & breeding
Chinese Goose Active birds with relatively good egg-producing potential. Eggs & breeding
African Goose Large type valued in some production systems for body size. Meat & breeding
Local / Adapted Geese May have useful adaptation to local environments and management systems. Local production
Important:

Breed selection should consider the local climate, available feed, water resources, disease risks, production objective, market demand, and availability of healthy breeding stock.

05

Goose Housing

Geese need a clean, dry, secure place where they can rest, shelter from harsh weather, and remain protected from predators.

Keep housing dry and well drained.
Provide good natural or mechanical ventilation.
Protect birds from predators and theft.
Provide clean and comfortable resting areas.
Use suitable litter and replace wet material.
Ensure adequate access to feeders and drinkers.
06

Gosling Brooding

Young goslings require special care during their early weeks. They need appropriate warmth, protection from drafts, clean bedding, easy access to feed, and clean drinking water.

Watch the birds:

Gosling behavior can provide useful clues about comfort. Crowding together may indicate that birds are too cold, while excessive spreading or panting may indicate heat stress.

07

Feeding & Nutrition 🌾

Good nutrition supports growth, immunity, reproduction, egg production, and overall performance.

Production Stage Main Feeding Focus
Goslings Balanced starter nutrition with easy access to clean water.
Growing Geese Balanced grower feed together with suitable forage where available.
Breeding Geese Balanced nutrition supporting body condition and reproduction.
Pasture-Based Birds Good-quality forage supplemented according to production needs.

Energy

Provides fuel for movement, growth, maintenance, and production.

Protein

Supports muscle development, tissue growth, feather development, and reproduction.

Water

Clean water must be continuously available and drinkers should be cleaned regularly.

Forage

Suitable pasture can provide valuable vegetation, but should not replace a balanced diet when birds require additional nutrients.

08

Water & Grazing Management 💧

Geese naturally spend time around water and can make excellent use of vegetation. However, access to water should be managed carefully to prevent excessive mud, contamination, and disease.

Provide clean drinking water every day.
Clean drinkers frequently.
Use safe and suitable grazing areas.
Prevent access to contaminated water sources.

Egg Collection & Handling 🥚

Eggs intended for hatching should be collected carefully and handled hygienically. Dirty, cracked, or damaged eggs should be managed appropriately according to their intended use.

Good practice:

Keep nests clean, collect eggs regularly, avoid unnecessary handling, and store hatching eggs under conditions suitable for maintaining their quality.

11

Common Goose Health Problems

Viral Diseases

Some viral diseases can cause serious losses in waterfowl and require professional veterinary attention.

Bacterial Infections

Bacterial problems may be associated with contaminated feed, water, housing, or poor hygiene.

Parasites

Internal and external parasites can affect growth, condition, and productivity.

Heat Stress

Hot conditions can reduce feed intake and increase water requirements.

Poor Ventilation

Moisture and poor air quality can contribute to respiratory and hygiene problems.

Biosecurity Risks

Contact with infected birds, contaminated equipment, people, or wild birds can introduce disease.

Veterinary guidance: If birds show unusual deaths, severe illness, neurological signs, breathing problems, diarrhea, or sudden production changes, contact a qualified veterinary professional promptly.
12

Biosecurity & Disease Prevention 🛡️

Control unnecessary visitors around the flock.
Keep footwear and equipment clean.
Reduce contact with wild birds where possible.
Control rodents and other disease-carrying pests.
Separate sick birds from healthy birds.
Dispose of dead birds safely and hygienically.
13

Goose Welfare

Good welfare is an important part of productive farming. Farmers should provide appropriate nutrition, clean water, suitable shelter, adequate space, protection from extreme weather, and prompt attention to sick or injured birds.

Clean Water

Birds should have reliable access to clean drinking water.

Good Nutrition

Feed should support the birds' age and production stage.

Suitable Shelter

Housing should protect birds from harsh weather and predators.

Health Monitoring

Farmers should observe birds regularly for changes in appetite, behavior, movement, and appearance.

14

Record Keeping & Farm Management

Accurate records help farmers understand production performance, identify problems early, control costs, and make better decisions.

Daily bird numbers and mortality.
Feed purchases and feed consumption.
Growth and body-weight observations.
Egg production and hatchability records.
Health treatments and veterinary records.
Farm expenses, sales, and income.
15

Products from Goose Farming

🥩 Goose Meat

The primary product in many goose production systems.

🥚 Goose Eggs

Large eggs can be sold for food or used for hatching.

🐣 Goslings

Healthy young birds can be sold to other farmers for growing or breeding.

🌱 Poultry Manure

Properly managed manure can contribute nutrients to crop production.

16

Advantages & Challenges

Potential Advantages

Grazing ability, multiple products, farm integration, hardy birds, and opportunities for meat and breeding markets.

Potential Challenges

Feed costs, disease risks, predator pressure, water management, market access, and the need for appropriate breeding stock.

17

Daily Goose Management Checklist

Observe birds for unusual behavior or illness.
Check clean drinking water.
Check feed availability and condition.
Inspect housing, fences, and gates.
Check pasture and grazing areas.
Remove wet or heavily contaminated bedding.
Monitor egg production and breeding activity.
Record important farm events.

Goose Farming at a Glance 🪿

Successful goose farming combines good genetics, proper nutrition, clean water, suitable housing, pasture management, biosecurity, animal welfare, breeding control, and accurate farm records.
