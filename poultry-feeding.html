🐔 Poultry Feeding & Nutrition

01

What is Poultry Feeding?

Poultry feeding is the practice of providing birds with suitable feed and water according to their species, age, production stage, health condition, and farming purpose.

Different poultry birds have different nutritional requirements. A chick, growing chicken, laying hen, broiler, turkey, duck, goose, or guinea fowl should not automatically receive exactly the same feeding program.

Good feeding means: The right nutrients + the right balance + the right production stage + safe feed + clean water.
02

Essential Nutrients for Poultry 🧬

Poultry require several major groups of nutrients to support growth, maintenance, reproduction, immunity, egg production, and meat production.

Protein

Supports muscle development, growth, feather formation, tissue repair, egg production, enzymes, hormones, and reproduction.

Energy

Provides fuel for movement, growth, maintenance, reproduction, egg production, and temperature regulation.

Fats & Oils

Provide concentrated energy and essential fatty acids when included appropriately in the diet.

Carbohydrates

Important energy sources commonly supplied by cereal grains and other suitable ingredients.

Vitamins

Support immunity, growth, bone development, reproduction, vision, metabolism, and health.

Minerals

Calcium, phosphorus, sodium, zinc, selenium, and other minerals support bones, eggshells, metabolism, and body functions.

Water

Essential for digestion, nutrient transport, temperature regulation, metabolism, waste removal, and production.

03

Feeding According to Bird Age 🐣

Nutritional requirements change as birds grow. Feeding programs should therefore change according to age and production stage.

🐣

Starter Stage

Supports early development through suitable starter nutrition, easy access to feed and water, hygiene, and careful observation.

🌱

Grower Stage

Supports healthy and controlled growth while monitoring body weight, feed intake, health, and flock uniformity.

🍗

Finisher Stage

Supports final growth and appropriate market performance in meat-producing birds.

🥚

Layer Stage

Supports egg production, eggshell quality, body maintenance, bone strength, and reproductive health.

🐣

Breeder Stage

Supports fertility, reproductive health, appropriate body condition, egg quality, and hatchability.

04

Main Poultry Feed Types 🍽️

Feed Type Main Purpose
Starter Feed Supports early growth and development.
Grower Feed Supports controlled growth and development.
Finisher Feed Supports final growth and meat production.
Layer Feed Supports egg production and eggshell quality.
Breeder Feed Supports reproduction, fertility, and hatchability.
Specialized Feed Designed for particular species or production systems.
05

Poultry Feeding by Species 🐔🦆🦃

🐔

Chicken

Feeding depends on whether chickens are raised for meat, eggs, breeding, or dual-purpose production.

🦃

Turkey

Turkey feeding should consider age, growth stage, breed, production objective, protein, energy, minerals, and water availability.

🦆

Duck

Ducks need diets appropriate for age and production purpose, with careful attention to clean drinking water.

🪿

Goose

Geese may use pasture and vegetation in suitable systems, complemented by appropriate supplemental feed.

🐦

Guinea Fowl

Young keets require careful starter nutrition, clean water, hygiene, and protection from competition.

06

Important Poultry Feed Ingredients 🌽

Energy Ingredients

Maize, sorghum, wheat, and other suitable cereals.

Protein Ingredients

Soybean meal, oilseed meals, fish meal where appropriate, and other safe protein sources.

Mineral Sources

Limestone, phosphate sources, salt, and mineral premixes.

Vitamin Sources

Appropriate vitamin premixes can help meet dietary requirements when properly formulated.

Other Ingredients

Oils, amino acids, enzymes, and other approved feed additives may be used according to nutritional needs.

07

Feed Form & Physical Quality 🥣

Poultry feed can be supplied in different physical forms. The appropriate form depends on bird age, species, production system, equipment, and farm objectives.

Mash Finely or coarsely ground feed ingredients.
Crumbles Smaller pieces of processed feed suitable for certain stages.
Pellets Compressed feed designed to provide a consistent ration.
Good feed should be: Fresh, dry, properly formulated, free from visible mold, free from pests, and stored safely.
08

Never Feed Poor-Quality Feed 🚫

Moldy feed
Rotten feed
Wet or spoiled feed
Insect-infested feed
Strongly rancid feed
Contaminated feed
Important: Moldy or contaminated feed can contain harmful substances such as mycotoxins and may negatively affect bird health, immunity, growth, and production.
09

Proper Feed Storage 🏠

Keep feed in a dry, clean area.
Protect feed from rain and moisture.
Keep bags off the floor where possible.
Control rodents and insects.
Keep storage areas ventilated.
Keep chemicals away from feed.
Clean spills immediately.
Identify different feed types clearly.
10

Rodent & Pest Protection 🐀

Rodents can consume feed and contaminate it with droppings, urine, hair, and potentially harmful microorganisms.

Seal Holes

Reduce access points around feed storage areas.

Protect Feed

Use suitable containers and protected storage.

Clean Spills

Remove spilled feed quickly.

Control Pests

Use safe and appropriate pest-management practices.

11

Water & Feeding Are Connected 💧

Feed intake and water intake are closely connected. If birds cannot access sufficient clean water, they may reduce feed consumption and production.

Clean Water

Provide safe and clean water continuously.

Clean Drinkers

Clean drinkers regularly and prevent contamination.

Check Equipment

Inspect drinkers, pipes, nipples, leaks, and blockages.

Monitor Consumption

Investigate unusual changes in water intake.

12

Feeding During Hot Weather 🌡️

Heat stress can change feeding behavior. Birds may eat less, drink more, pant, become less active, and experience reduced production.

Reliable clean water
Good ventilation
Shade where appropriate
Reduce environmental heat
Appropriate stocking density
Monitor feed intake
13

Feed Additives & Supplements 🧪

Some poultry diets may contain additives or supplements designed for specific nutritional or production purposes.

Vitamin & mineral premixes
Amino acids
Enzymes
Approved probiotic products
Remember: Not every flock needs every supplement. Use should be based on the diet, bird requirements, product instructions, and qualified nutritional or veterinary advice where appropriate.
14

Locally Available Feed Resources 🌱

Smallholder farmers may use locally available ingredients where these are safe, nutritionally suitable, properly processed, and correctly included in a balanced ration.

Maize
Sorghum
Suitable agricultural by-products
Suitable insect protein
Soybean products
Other suitable feed resources
Important: A cheap ingredient is not automatically a good ingredient. Nutrient value, digestibility, safety, availability, storage, cost, and contamination risk must all be considered.
15

Balanced Ration ⚖️

A balanced ration supplies nutrients in appropriate proportions for the bird's requirements.

Energy
+
Protein
+
Amino Acids
+
Minerals
+
Vitamins
+
Water
The goal is balance. Providing a large quantity of one nutrient does not replace the need for the other nutrients required by the bird.
16

Feed Intake & Farm Records 📊

Farmers should record feed use whenever possible. Good records help identify changes early and understand flock performance.

Feed Purchased Record quantity and type of feed purchased.
Feed Used Monitor how much feed is consumed.
Production Compare feed use with eggs, growth, or other output.
Feed Cost Track feed expenses as part of farm management.
17

Feed Conversion & Farm Efficiency 📈

Feed conversion describes how efficiently feed is converted into production. For meat birds, this can be considered in relation to weight gain. For egg enterprises, farmers can monitor feed use in relation to egg production.

Monitor Growth

Track body weight and growth performance where appropriate.

Monitor Production

Compare feed use with egg production or other output.

Investigate Changes

Look for disease, water, feed, housing, or environmental problems.

Prevent Feed Wastage 🚫

Adjust feeders correctly.
Avoid overfilling feeders.
Keep feeders clean.
Protect feed from rodents.
Monitor daily feed consumption.
Store feed correctly.

Feeding Young Birds 🐣

Make feed easy to find and reach.
Provide clean water consistently.
Use an appropriate starter diet.
Keep feeding equipment clean.
Monitor weaker birds.
Maintain suitable brooding conditions.
20

Feeding Laying Birds 🥚

Laying birds require nutrition that supports both egg production and body maintenance.

Energy Supports production and body maintenance.
Protein Supports egg production and body tissues.
Calcium Important for eggshell formation and bone health.
Phosphorus Works with calcium in bone and eggshell metabolism.
Vitamins & Minerals Support overall health and production.
Clean Water Essential for normal production and health.
21

Feeding Meat Birds 🍗

Meat birds need nutrition that supports healthy growth and efficient production while maintaining appropriate welfare and environmental conditions.

Monitor body weight.
Monitor growth rate.
Monitor feed intake.
Monitor water intake.
Monitor bird health.
Maintain appropriate housing.
22

Feed Hygiene 🧼

Feed hygiene is an important part of flock health. Feeders, storage areas, and feeding equipment should be kept clean and protected from contamination.

Clean feeders regularly.
Prevent fecal contamination.
Remove spoiled feed.
Keep feed dry.
Protect feed from pests.
Keep storage areas clean.
23

Changing Feed Safely 🔄

Feeding programs should change according to the bird's production stage. Unnecessary sudden changes can disrupt normal feeding behavior or performance.

02 Check the Bird's Age
03 Check Production Stage
04 Review Nutritional Specifications
05 Monitor the Flock

Signs of Possible Feeding or Nutrition Problems ⚠️

Poor growth
Sudden reduction in feed intake
Poor feather development
Weakness
Poor body condition
Reduced egg production
Poor eggshell quality
Uneven flock growth
Increased feed wastage
Unusual changes in water consumption
Important: These signs do not automatically mean the problem is nutritional. Disease, parasites, heat stress, poor housing, water problems, and other factors can produce similar signs.

Sustainable Poultry Feeding 🌱

Sustainable feeding means using resources efficiently while maintaining bird health, welfare, productivity, and environmental responsibility.

Reduce feed wastage.
Use suitable local ingredients where appropriate.
Prevent feed contamination.
Monitor feed efficiency.
Protect water resources.
Manage manure responsibly.
27

Golden Rules of Poultry Feeding 🌾

01 Feed according to the bird, not simply according to what is available.
02 Clean water is essential.
03 Never knowingly feed moldy or contaminated feed.
04 Store feed in a clean, dry, protected environment.
05 Prevent feed wastage.
06 Monitor feed intake and production together.
07 Change diets according to the production stage.
08 Do not assume every poultry species has the same nutritional requirements.
09 Investigate sudden changes in feeding or production.
10 Seek qualified professional advice when nutritional problems are suspected.

Better Feeding. Healthier Birds. Stronger Farms. 🐔🌱

Successful poultry feeding is not simply about giving birds enough food. It is about providing the right nutrients, in the right balance, at the right stage, with safe feed and continuous access to clean water.
