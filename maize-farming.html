🌽 Maize Farming: The Complete Guide from Planting to Harvest

Learn modern maize production techniques that help improve yields, increase profits, and promote sustainable farming across Africa.

View Maize Guide

Introduction

Maize is one of Africa's most important food and cash crops. It provides daily meals for millions of families, supports livestock production, supplies raw materials to industries, and generates income for farmers across the continent. Whether you are a beginner starting your first maize field or an experienced farmer looking to improve productivity, success begins with good planning and the consistent application of modern farming practices. High maize yields are not achieved by chance. They result from choosing the right seed, preparing fertile land, planting at the correct time, managing weeds and pests effectively, and harvesting properly. This guide explains every important stage of maize production—from choosing land to safely storing harvested grain.

📌 Quick Facts About Maize

🌦 Climate

Best rainfall: 500–1,200 mm

Ideal temperature: 18°C–30°C

🌱 Soil

Deep, fertile and well-drained soils rich in organic matter.

Best pH: 5.5–7.0

🌾 Planting

Row spacing: 75 cm

Plant spacing: 25–30 cm

🚜 Harvest

Usually harvested after physiological maturity when grains are hard and dry.

🌍 Choosing the Right Land

Choosing the right field is the foundation of successful maize farming. Even the highest-quality seed cannot produce excellent yields if planted in unsuitable soil. Maize performs best in fertile, deep, and well-drained soils that allow roots to grow freely while retaining enough moisture during the growing season. Fields that become waterlogged after heavy rain should be avoided because standing water damages roots, limits oxygen, and encourages diseases. Before planting, inspect your land carefully. Remove large stones, tree stumps, and other obstacles that interfere with cultivation. Where possible, conduct a soil test to determine nutrient levels and soil acidity. This helps farmers apply the correct fertilizer instead of guessing. Good land selection reduces production costs and improves crop performance throughout the season.

🌽 Selecting the Right Seed Variety

Seed selection has one of the greatest influences on maize production. Always purchase certified seed from trusted suppliers rather than using unknown grain from local markets. Certified seed offers higher germination rates, improved resistance to major diseases, better drought tolerance in suitable varieties, and higher yield potential. When selecting a variety, consider:

  • Rainfall patterns in your area.
  • Length of the growing season.
  • Resistance to pests and diseases.
  • Expected grain yield.
  • Market demand.
  • Availability of irrigation.

Consult agricultural extension officers or certified seed companies to identify varieties that perform well in your region.

🚜 Land Preparation

Proper land preparation creates favorable conditions for seed germination and root development. Prepare the field several weeks before planting by removing weeds, previous crop residues, and unwanted vegetation. The field should then be ploughed to loosen compacted soil before harrowing to produce a fine seedbed. Where conservation agriculture is practiced, minimum tillage may be used to reduce soil erosion and preserve soil moisture. Good land preparation improves water infiltration, reduces weed competition, and encourages uniform crop establishment.

🌱 Planting Maize

Plant maize immediately after the first effective rains or when adequate irrigation water is available. Recommended spacing:

  • Row spacing: 75 cm
  • Plant spacing: 25–30 cm
  • Planting depth: 3–5 cm

Correct spacing allows each plant to receive enough sunlight, nutrients, water, and air circulation while reducing competition between plants. Timely planting is one of the simplest ways to increase maize yields.
