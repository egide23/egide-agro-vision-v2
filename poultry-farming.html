01 / Introduction

What Is Poultry Farming?

Poultry farming involves raising domesticated birds for meat, eggs, breeding stock and other agricultural products. Important poultry species include chickens, ducks, turkeys, geese and guinea fowl.

In Rwanda, chicken production is an important opportunity for household nutrition, rural employment and agribusiness. Other poultry species may also offer opportunities where suitable markets and production conditions exist.

Successful poultry farming requires proper housing, reliable feed, clean water, health monitoring, biosecurity, financial planning and market access.

Remember: Different poultry species have different nutritional, housing, breeding and health requirements. Always follow species-specific guidance.

03 / Good Farming Practices

Good Management Creates Better Results

Healthy poultry production starts with good planning and continues through every stage of flock management.

Build a Healthy Poultry Enterprise

Prepare suitable housing, reliable feed and water supplies, and a health-management plan before bringing birds to your farm.

  • Provide appropriate housing and ventilation.
  • Ensure continuous access to clean drinking water.
  • Feed birds according to species and growth stage.
  • Apply suitable biosecurity measures.
  • Consult veterinary professionals about vaccination.
  • Keep mortality, production, expense and sales records.
Learn Poultry Management
Advice for farmers in Rwanda: Consult local veterinary and agricultural extension services about appropriate breeds, disease prevention, vaccination and locally approved veterinary products.
