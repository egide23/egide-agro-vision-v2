Why Ruminant Farming Matters

Ruminant livestock can contribute to food production, household income, manure resources, employment, and agricultural resilience. Good management helps farmers improve animal welfare, productivity, and resource use.

Sustainable Production Promote responsible use of land, feed, water, and other farm resources.
Animal Health Strong health and preventive management support productive and healthy herds and flocks.
Farm Productivity Good records and management can help farmers understand production and improve efficiency.
